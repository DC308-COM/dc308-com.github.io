**DEFCON 308** is the local DEFCON group for Western Nebraska (Area Code 308).

We are a community of security researchers, hackers, engineers, sysadmins, students, and hobbyists dedicated to sharing knowledge about cybersecurity, hardware hacking, reverse engineering, software craftsmanship, and privacy.

### What is a DEFCON Group?

DEFCON Groups (DCGs) are independent, local gatherings of people interested in security, technology, and hacker culture. Anyone with an interest in technology, cybersecurity, and making or breaking things is welcome.

- **Open to All:** No fees, no dues, no gatekeeping.
- **Hands-on & Practical:** Demos, presentations, capture-the-flag (CTF) challenges, and open discussions.
